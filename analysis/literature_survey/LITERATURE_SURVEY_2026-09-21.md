# Literature survey: what studies of low-Z / organic / biological materials do (2026-09-21)

Method: (i) the 100 most recent RPC shielding titles in `~/CHON/reference` classified by keyword; (ii) a CrossRef survey (18 queries, 2015-2026, 678 unique records, 490 with photon-attenuation content; abstracts available for ~1/3) classified by keyword; (iii) abstracts of the closest studies read via CrossRef / web search. Keyword statistics are approximate; the search is not exhaustive; full texts were not read.

Key numbers
- RPC recent 100 shielding titles: glass 37, concrete/ceramic/alloy/metal 33, polymer composites 30 (almost all filler-reinforced), neutron 17, organic/biological only 2.
- CrossRef set: 175 of 490 mention low-Z-type materials; only 90 without high-Z / boron / oxide / glass wording; 80 of 126 polymer records mention a high-Z filler.
- Features among records with abstracts (n=183): XCOM/Phy-X/PSD 40%, experiment 59%, Geant4 8%, MAC/HVL/MFP 63%, Zeff/Ne 31%, buildup 13%, filler/density/thickness effects 69%, composition-dependence analysis (correlation/sensitivity/element contribution) 8%.
